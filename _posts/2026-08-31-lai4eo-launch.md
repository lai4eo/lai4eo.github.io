We are excited to launch the LAI4EO consortium and invite the community to learn more about our work and meet the team.

The LAI4EO starter repository is now available on Zenodo: [https://zenodo.org/records/21246927](https://zenodo.org/records/21246927). It provides an entry point for the consortium’s resources, datasets, and collaborations, and reflects the growing community around in-situ LAI measurements for Earth observation applications.

The consortium will be presented at the upcoming [RAQRS conference in Valencia, Spain](https://ipl.uv.es/raqrs/), from September 21–25, 2026, and at [AGU in San Francisco](https://www.agu.org/annual-meeting) from December 7–11, 2026. These meetings will be a great opportunity to learn about the work being done, exchange ideas, and connect with researchers, data providers, and practitioners working across the LAI and Earth observation community.

We warmly encourage everyone to come find us, stop by, and talk with us. We are eager to share the vision behind LAI4EO, discuss collaboration opportunities, and hear from others who are interested in advancing this work.
