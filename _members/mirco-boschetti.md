Dr. Boschetti is a senior researcher at the National Research Council of Italy, usinng remote sensing for vegetation monitoring and the definition of environmental indicator through geographic multisource data integration. He is a member of the European Journal of Remote Sensing Scientific Board. Participated to several national (Ministry of Environment, Italian Space Agency) and international programs (EC, ESA).
