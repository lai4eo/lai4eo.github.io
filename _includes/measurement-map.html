{% comment %} Map of the LAI4EO sites, clustered. One marker per site, labelled with its measurement count and coloured by its dominant crop. Leaflet.markercluster merges the ones that overlap at the current zoom, and a cluster's label is the SUM of its children's counts - not the number of markers, which would read "3" over a region holding 1,100 measurements. Zoom in and the clusters split into the sites they hold. Every circle on this map, clustered or not, is a measurement count. That is the whole point of the design, and it is why sites are div icons rather than Leaflet circleMarkers: a bare circle at one zoom and a numbered bubble at the next is two visual languages for one thing. It does NOT plot measurements: an individual field position is private data, and nothing here is finer than a cluster centroid rounded to ~1 km. See guide/REJECTED.md - both "raw points on the map" and "un-clustered circles" are rejected rows, and this file is the replacement for the second. Sites come from assets/data/measurements.json, generated by tools/build_site_data.py alongside _data/sites.yaml - the two always agree because they are written from the same rows.
